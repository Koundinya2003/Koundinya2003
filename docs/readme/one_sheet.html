01Research interests

The questions that pull me in are about the mind's logical architecture.

MEMORY

Slots or a resource?

Why does working memory hold so little, and is its limit a fixed number of slots or a shared resource?

ATTENTION

One capacity, many inputs

How does attention allocate a limited capacity across competing inputs?

DECISION

Sensitivity or bias?

How do decisions emerge from noisy evidence, and how can signal detection theory separate sensitivity from bias?

Why I can do it: my background in Computer Science and Engineering gives me formal logic, probability, algorithms and code for simulation and statistical modelling.

02Featured research

The Song or the Self? Personal musical emotion in scalp EEG.

Within individuals, is self reported emotion during music listening linked to EEG band power, beyond what every listener shares for the same song? Preregistered before any results were seen.

20listeners
20songs
66EEG channels
395clean trials

The key move: split each rating

Valence
49% song
47% self
Arousal
67% song
29%

song consensus (the other listeners) personal deviation

MET dataset, OpenNeuro ds008701. R, lme4, mixed effects models with random intercepts for person and song.

Three preregistered bets

H1

More aroused than usual, lower posterior alpha

NOT SUPPORTED
H2

More pleasant than usual, more left frontal activity

NOT SUPPORTED
H3

People differ in these links

NOT SUPPORTED

0 of 3 supported, and none of 105 exploratory tests survived correction.

An honest null, reported in full with its limitations.
